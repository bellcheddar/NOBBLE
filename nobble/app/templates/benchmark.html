Benchmark panel

Seven pairs with published crystallographic ternaries, run first in every campaign. This is the page a sceptical reader opens first, so it reports what each pair tests, what the pipeline predicted and where it disagreed with the deposited structure. Every accession, chain and ligand code below was resolved programmatically at build time: none is hardcoded.

GSPT1 and BRD4 lead, as community standards. BRD4 appears twice, on VHL and on CRBN, which tests an E3 swap on the same substrate.

Construct comparison: does the whole domain recover the pose?

Six of the seven pairs submitted their substrate as a 15-mer cut out of a folded domain, and all six missed the crystal pose by 12 to 29 angstrom. The one whose epitope is linear, IKZF1, recovered at 5.4. This re-runs the panel with each domain-mediated substrate submitted as its whole domain and changes nothing else.

IKZF1 is the control. It keeps its 15-mer in both campaigns, so if its recovery moves, something other than the construct changed and nothing below can be attributed to the construct.
