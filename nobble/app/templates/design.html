Design arm

Everything else in NOBBLE measures whether a predicted interface tells you something true. This is the part that proposes something new: variants of degrons with a known experimental outcome, scored by the same pipeline, to ask which positions a degron depends on and whether any change improves it.

Every variant is anchored to a parent. The headline number is design lift, delta-ipTM(variant) minus delta-ipTM(parent): a difference of differences, which cancels whatever the receptor, the glue and the parent degron each contribute, and leaves the effect of the one substitution.
