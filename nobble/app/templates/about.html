What NOBBLE measures

Does the glue change the predicted interface, and does that change separate degraded substrates from non-degraded ones in the same screen?

delta_iptm = iptm_min_ternary - iptm_min_binary

Measured on the receptor-to-peptide chain pair, not global ipTM: the global figure averages in the receptor's own well-predicted core.

Bridge formation: reported, not weighted

How many seeds produced a glue touching both chains at all. Independent of the composite and of any crystal, so it separates a weak interface from no interface: every failed bridge in the panel failed the same way, the glue reaching one chain rather than two.

It carries no weight in the composite and does not decide the corrected-construct verdict, which stays on pose RMSD. It is reported beside both.

The confidence caveat, stated plainly

{# Spec 4.5 and the Phase 4 list both ask for this caveat on the About page, and it was here with "poorly" where the spec gives a number. A measured range beats an adjective, and it is five words (F49). #}

Boltz-2 on 519 therapeutic antibodies separates cognate from non-cognate antigens at ROC-AUC 0.5 to 0.60, with ipTM-min the strongest of the confidence metrics. ipTM is enrichment, not a verdict.

On the first benchmark pair a bridging pose scored below a non-bridging one, so geometry leads the ranking.

The design arm, and what it is not

The only part that proposes rather than measures, and not in the build specification. It ships before the score it ranks by is validated.

QuestionAnswer
What is the number? Design lift: delta-ipTM(variant) minus delta-ipTM(parent). A difference of differences, so the receptor, the glue and the parent degron all cancel
What does it claim? The ordering of positions. That survives a monotone miscalibration of the scale; "this peptide will be degraded" does not, and is not claimed
When does it refuse? Different receptor, E3, ligand or modality between a variant and its parent, fewer than three seeds on either side, or a substitution of a residue for itself. No lift is returned at all, rather than an approximate one
Why is nothing scored yet? The scan runs after the main campaign. Running it first would produce lifts against an unvalidated score that would have to be rerun
