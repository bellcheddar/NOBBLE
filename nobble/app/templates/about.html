What NOBBLE measures

NOBBLE generates and scores peptide poses against curated targeted-protein-degradation machinery using Boltz-2 ternary co-folding, and asks one question of every pair: does the presence of the glue or degrader change the predicted interface, and does that change separate experimentally degraded substrates from experimentally non-degraded ones assayed in the same screen? 

delta_iptm = iptm_min_ternary - iptm_min_binary

Measured on the receptor-to-peptide chain pair, not the global ipTM: on a ternary complex the global figure averages in the receptor-to-ligand interface, which is the easy one, so a glue sitting perfectly in its pocket while the degron fails to dock would raise it.

The confidence caveat, stated plainly

The Thera-SAbDab benchmark of Boltz-2 across 519 therapeutic antibodies paired with cognate and non-cognate antigens found that its confidence metrics gave only weak, though above-chance, discrimination: ROC-AUC between 0.5 and 0.60, with ipTM-min carrying the strongest signal and multivariate modelling adding essentially nothing. Peptide-protein should fare better than antibody-antigen, but this application does not present ipTM as a verdict. It is reported as enrichment, alongside the geometry.

That is not a theoretical caution. On the benchmark panel's first pair, a pose that genuinely bridged receptor to peptide scored ipTM 0.885 and one that did not scored 0.877; the highest ipTM in the whole ternary set, 0.916, did not bridge at all. The bridge percentile separated them; ipTM did not.

Lysine reach: why there is no score

The specification asks for BINMAN's reach-window fit, described as already calibrated against 976 observed diGly sites. It is not calibrated. BINMAN attempted the fit, measured it and declined to ship it, and these are the numbers behind that decision:

All at or below chance, and the Cb-Cb direction is slightly inverted: ubiquitylated lysines sit farther from the ligand ({{ reach_auc.ubiquitylated_median_cb_cb }} A median) than unmodified ones ({{ reach_auc.unmodified_median_cb_cb }} A). The explanation is mechanistic rather than technical, which is why refitting is unlikely to help: the ligand site is where a substrate is recruited, not where ubiquitin is transferred, and the E2 that transfers it sits elsewhere on the cullin scaffold and is not in the trimmed construct at all. NOBBLE therefore reports lysine geometry as a measurement and emits no reach score.

The design arm, and what it is not

The Design tab is the only part of NOBBLE that proposes something rather than measuring something, and it is deliberately not part of the build specification. Everything else here asks whether a predicted interface is real; that tab asks which positions a known degron depends on.

It ships before the score it would rank by is validated, which is worth stating on the page a sceptical reader opens. The matched-control ROC above reports not_computed until the Sievers arm runs, so a ranked list of invented peptides would be a recommendation drawn from a scale whose discrimination has never been measured.

QuestionAnswer
What is the number? Design lift: delta-ipTM(variant) minus delta-ipTM(parent). A difference of differences, so the receptor, the glue and the parent degron all cancel
What does it claim? The ordering of positions. That survives a monotone miscalibration of the scale; "this peptide will be degraded" does not, and is not claimed
When does it refuse? Different receptor, E3, ligand or modality between a variant and its parent, fewer than three seeds on either side, or a substitution of a residue for itself. No lift is returned at all, rather than an approximate one
Why is nothing scored yet? The scan runs after the main campaign. Running it first would produce lifts against an unvalidated score that would have to be rerun
